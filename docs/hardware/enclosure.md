# Enclosure notes

## Dimensions

| Part | Size | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Panel outline | 170.2 x 111.2 x 0.91 mm | Waveshare wiki |
| Active area | 160.0 x 96.0 mm | Waveshare wiki |
| Driver board | 65.0 x 30.5 mm | Waveshare wiki |
| FireBeetle 2 ESP32-S3 | not confirmed | check the DFRobot wiki |

## Suggestion

A slanted picture-frame case, about 70 degrees from horizontal, printed in two parts:
- Front bezel with a window slightly under 160 x 96 mm of active area plus margin for the 170.2 x 111.2 mm outline.
- Rear shell holding the driver board, the FireBeetle and the LiPo, with the USB-C port reachable from the side or back.
- Three buttons on the front edge or below the window, mapped to approve, reject and stop (left to right).
- Leave room for the FFC cable bend and a vent or antenna clearance near the ESP32 module.
